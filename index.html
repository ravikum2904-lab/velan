About Us

Safe, Fun & Educational – Just What Your Child Needs

At Shree Velan Play School, Sirumugai, we believe every child deserves a happy, safe, and inspiring beginning to their learning journey. Our aim is to create a warm and welcoming environment where little minds can explore, learn, play, and grow with confidence. Through fun-filled activities and engaging learning experiences, we encourage creativity, curiosity, and the overall development of every child.
